## Result

A three-state cyclic hidden Markov system with a noisy binary observation channel produces six distinct posterior histories and six distinct future response profiles, but its centered linear predictive Hankel matrix has rank two.

The exact observation model is:
\[
P(1\mid x)=(0.9,0.5,0.1),\qquad
P(0\mid x)=(0.1,0.5,0.9),
\]
with uniform prior and cyclic actions on \(\mathbb Z_3\).

For the six histories (observation, then action), the posterior beliefs are all distinct. The history-future probability matrix factors exactly:
\[
H_{h,\tau}=P(\tau\mid h)=\sum_x\mu_h(x)P(\tau\mid x)
=(RO)_{h,\tau}.
\]

After centering and prior whitening, the posterior-history Gramian and future-test Gramian are:
\[
W_c=\frac{16}{75}P_\perp,\qquad
W_o=\frac{4}{75}P_\perp,
\]
where \(P_\perp=I-\frac13\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top\).

Therefore:
\[
W_c^{1/2}W_oW_c^{1/2}
=\frac{64}{5625}P_\perp.
\]
Its two nonzero eigenvalues are both \(64/5625\approx0.0113777778\). These match the two nonzero squared singular values of the weighted Hankel matrix. The remaining eigenvalues are zero up to floating-point roundoff.

By contrast, the action-only uniform-prior past Gramian remains zero: all cyclic actions preserve the uniform prior. Observations are what create the six distinct posterior states.

## Interpretation

This is a stronger confirmation of the posterior-history factorization than Spike 23:
- the observer's predictive state is its posterior over hidden physical states;
- the six reachable posterior states are distinct as exact predictive states;
- the linear predictive dimension is only two;
- the balanced Gram product reproduces the weighted Hankel spectrum exactly.

This is a concrete illustration of the difference between the cardinality of the exact semantic quotient and the dimension of a linear realization. A finite predictive quotient can have more states than the minimal linear predictive representation requires.

The result also identifies the proper past feature map for an agent: posterior distributions generated by actual action-observation histories, not only the action-induced propagation of the initial prior.

## Limits

This is a finite synthetic HMM, not a new general theorem. The Hankel factorization and balanced Gram identity are standard finite-dimensional realization mathematics. The Semplex contribution is to make the observer's information history explicit in the Master Principle's past operator and to provide a verified bridge fixture for subsequent generalization.
